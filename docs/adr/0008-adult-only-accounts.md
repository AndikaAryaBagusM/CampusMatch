# Accounts are for people aged 18 or over

UU PDP (UU No. 27/2022) requires parental or guardian consent to process a child's personal data, and many Tes Minat users are SMA/SMK students aged 15–17. Rather than build a parental-consent flow for the MVP, **every account requires declaring "Saya berusia 18 tahun atau lebih"** at sign-up, stored as a yes/no with the time it was given. This covers Pengulas as well as people saving a Profil Minat. Under-18s can still take the Tes Minat without an account: the result lives only in its shareable link, which carries the scores in the URL, so we store nothing about them.

A Profil Minat stores only the six Profil RIASEC scores and the date (not the item answers), is private, can be deleted by its owner, and is never used for Promosi or ad targeting.

## Consequences

- Accounts created before this rule must make the declaration at their next sign-in before doing anything else. If someone declares they are under 18, the account is locked and they are shown how to request deletion; their Ulasan stay Terbit, since Ulasan are shown anonymously.
- Some first-year students are 17 and cannot write an Ulasan until they turn 18.
- A parental-consent flow is deferred until the lawyer has answered question 3 in [legal-todo.md](../legal-todo.md).
